# Research status and continuation

The repository is available for inspection and further research. Final scientific validation is incomplete; reproducible files do not establish that the review's conclusions are ready for submission or expert circulation as settled findings.

Work to complete before presenting the main findings as final:

1. Resolve flow scope from case-specific evidence, including the distinction between a compressible solver and a compressible flow. Recode Agrawal against the appropriate published/accepted text: an accepted-manuscript check has already found material changes from the coded preprint.
2. Reconsider the two studies excluded entirely for unknown scope, and document the denominator and eligibility decisions for a complete sensitivity analysis.
3. Reconcile repeated comparison units, including the two coded speed-bump pressure rows for N418.
4. Reassess all favourable/adverse pairs affected by scope, including N357, N358, N418, N626 and P19. Keep improvement relative to a baseline distinct from absolute agreement with a reference.
5. Recompute downstream contrasts, reporting denominators, manuscript tables and headline statements after those decisions. The current public sensitivity recomputes categories and exposure only.
6. Audit source support for claims of absence, uncertainty resolution and isolated causes. A coded unknown is not an absence, and a model can fail inside its calibration range.
7. Review the retrospective checklist's independence, case selection and version differences before claiming predictive validation. The current package supplies its baseline results and renders its figures; it does not rerun that assessment.
8. Check each outgoing factual question against the cited section and the corrected analysis. Use current contact details and respect the owner's separate instructions for correspondence and submission.

The baseline files remain available to show what changed. Corrections should identify affected study/test IDs, cite the exact source version and location, record the previous and revised codes, explain the decision, and recompute dependent results. Do not silently replace a historical snapshot or promote a sensitivity scenario to a corrected analysis.
