# Current research status

The current manuscript is a corrected critical review prepared for expert feedback. It presents source-checked examples, a physical framework and a proposed checklist. It does not present the historical corpus as a settled quantitative analysis.

Completed corrections are documented in [scientific-corrections.md](scientific-corrections.md): source/version reconciliation for retained cases, explicit physical-scope uncertainty, separation of relative and absolute error, withdrawal of unsupported aggregates, and a limited interpretation of retrospective checklist records.

The source reassessment traces every retrieved work. For the standard periodic hill at bulk Reynolds number 10,595, five studies tabulate separation and reattachment against a reference for both crossings. A direction of change is called resolved when it holds for every value that rounds to the printed digits and against every reference in the table. Of the nine fixed-mesh wall-treatment comparisons, five move both crossings closer, one is resolved in opposite directions (averaging interval not reported), one is unresolved (a one-decimal baseline and a reference-dependent reattachment change), one compares runs with different averaging windows and one has no baseline bubble. Ranking the point values alone gives five, three and one. In each of the three nominally opposite cases the smaller change is at most 0.05 hill heights. The studies reuse meshes, measurements and trained treatments. These tables do not establish a trade-off between the two crossings. A second automated reading re-coded 36 of the 115 works and 34 of the 79 earlier pairings from the rules and the sources alone. It agreed with the first reading on eligibility (34 of 36), on the coupled-LES, RANS, prescribed-input, system-benchmark and thermal groups (Cohen's κ from 0.89 to 0.94) and on whether two quantities came from the same pair of runs (31 of 34). It did not reproduce the membership of the matched wall-treatment-change group (κ 0.67, with six further works found), the constitutive-constraint group (κ 0.48), the three tests labelled as independent transfers, the number of design series per paper (71 against 179 for the same works) or the finer categories of same-run pairings (12 of 24). ## Limits of the current work

- The collection is not exhaustive and its historical coding is not a certified census of current journal versions.
- Identified preprints are used as preprints; journal equivalence is not assumed.
- The framework is not an exhaustive or mutually exclusive causal taxonomy.
- Retrospective proposal agreement does not validate the checklist's predictive value.
- Absolute accuracy requires a relevant reference and uncertainty assessment; sensitivity alone cannot supply it.
- Resolved directions are resolved to printed digits. Sampling, discretisation and measurement uncertainties enter only where a source reports them.
- The matched wall-treatment-change and constitutive-constraint groups need the clarified rules applied to all 115 works before any count is quoted.
- The journal tables of Krank and Wall (2016) and Zhou and Bae (2024) were not accessible. Their values come from the preprints named in the reference list; the first was also checked against the first author's dissertation.

The present scientific claims stand on their stated evidence and limitations. Reproduction checks do not constitute independent scientific replication or journal review.
