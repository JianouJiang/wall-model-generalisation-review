One OpenAlex~\cite{Priem2022} search per treatment class on \nSearchDate{} (journal articles, 2000--2026) kept
\nScreened{} records, including \nKnownFound{} of \nKnown{} studies listed in advance to test recall~\cite{Sampson2006}. Titles
and abstracts were screened; a second, blind pass screened a random fifth (Cohen's kappa \nKappa{},
Gwet's AC1 \nScreenAc{}~\cite{Wongpakaran2013}). Of \nForwarded{} records sent to a full-text check, \nRoute{} had a
candidate route to a lawful copy and \nRetrieved{} were retrieved (\nAccRecent{}\,\% of 2018--2026 records had a
route, \nAccEarly{}\,\% of 2000--2009). \nClaims{} met the inclusion rules: \nPilot{} pilot studies,
used to develop the coding rules, and \nClaimsNew{} coded after the pilot. Two independent passes coded
every study from its full text, test designs first from outcome-masked extracts. For those \nClaimsNew{}, \nMaskListed{} extracts still held abstract or conclusions text,
\nAdjItems{} disagreements were adjudicated from the source and \nUndecided{} values stayed uncertain; an uncertain
value of a counted test gives a range (Table~\ref{tab:evidence}).
