The examples were selected to distinguish physical and numerical explanations for errors in wall stress, separation or reattachment. An OpenAlex search of journal records from 2000 to 16 November 2026 supplied the initial literature. We also consulted earlier work on wall laws, studies of numerical error and papers cited by the selected authors.

Figure~\ref{fig:record} groups eight studies by flow geometry. For each comparison we identify the measured quantity, how the wall stress was supplied and which aspects of the calculation changed together. Where the plotted values come from a preprint or an accepted manuscript, the bibliography identifies the version used.
