The literature collection began with an OpenAlex search of journal records in 2000--2026 and lawful full-text retrieval. Historical screening and coding files are retained for traceability, including a set of 82 selected study treatments. They are not used here as a representative or fully reconciled quantitative sample.

The revision checked source versions, scope exclusions, comparison definitions, duplicated comparison units and retrospective independence. It examined all 33 records outside that historical set at the eligibility level and rechecked the passages supporting the cases retained in this paper. Eligibility checks are not complete re-extractions of those studies. Inconsistent boundaries between applications and independent tests, and between thermal and hydrodynamic evidence, preclude a repaired population denominator from a scope sensitivity alone.

The current synthesis is a critical, case-based review. Published versions are used for the Agrawal, Dupuy, building-block, reinforcement-learning and dynamic-slip examples. Other detailed case comparisons identify the specific preprint or accepted manuscript used, without asserting equivalence to the journal article. No conclusion of missing validation is inferred from a scope exclusion, and no field-wide success, failure or validation-deficit rate is reported.
