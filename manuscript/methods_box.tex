Four OpenAlex title-and-abstract searches covered RANS wall functions, LES wall conditions, inner--outer models and learned stress estimators (1 January 2000 to 16 November 2026). The accompanying protocol gives the strings, journal and language filters and deduplication by OpenAlex ID, DOI, then title and year. Of 2,543 screened records, 735 went forward, 171 had a candidate public retrieval route and 120 texts were obtained. The 115 reports reassessed here comprise 112 passing the full-text language check and three recorded language exclusions. Indexing and access limit coverage.

For a hydrodynamic comparison we require a named wall treatment or stress estimator, an identifiable test and a measured quantity with a comparator. Our primary scope is turbulent flow of a Newtonian fluid over smooth walls under incompressible or low-Mach conditions. In each report we distinguish flow, forcing, model and solver, then record observable, reference and use in calibration. Editions of one work count once. Runs reusing a flow, treatment or benchmark remain linked. We distinguish a restriction on admissible stress, a response to prescribed inputs, and a coupled LES or RANS result. A benchmark of either complete solver isolates a wall-treatment change only with matched controls. A heat-flux comparison has its own physical scope. A channel LES resolving the viscous sublayer with no wall treatment~\cite{Tsubokura2009} is a flow comparison only.

For comparisons of relative accuracy, we identify both calculations, then evaluate the change in discrepancy against a common reference within each quantity on stated spatial support. We traced 79 earlier pairings to their run and figure identities and recorded additional comparisons found in the collection. Those 79 were selected for heterogeneous results and cannot supply the frequency of reversals. Physical uncertainty is kept distinct from rounding and graphical reading precision. A second automated reading of 36 reports and 34 pairings, given only rules and sources, agreed on the coupled-LES, RANS, prescribed-input, system-benchmark and thermal groups (Cohen's $\kappa$ 0.89--0.94) and on 31 of 34 same-run judgements. It agreed less on matched wall-treatment changes ($\kappa=0.67$), finer same-run categories (12 of 24) and independent transfers, for which we quote no counts.
