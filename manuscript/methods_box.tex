Four OpenAlex title-and-abstract searches covered RANS wall functions, LES wall conditions, inner--outer models and learned stress estimators (1 January 2000 to 16 November 2026). The accompanying protocol gives the strings, journal and language filters and deduplication by OpenAlex ID, DOI, then title and year. Of 2,543 screened records, 735 went forward, 171 had a candidate public retrieval route and 120 texts were obtained. The 115 listed reports comprise 112 passing the full-text language check and three recorded language exclusions. Indexing and access limit coverage.

For a hydrodynamic comparison we require a named wall treatment or stress estimator, an identifiable test and a measured quantity with a comparator. Our primary scope is turbulent flow of a Newtonian fluid over smooth walls under incompressible or low-Mach conditions. Within each report we distinguish flow, forcing, model and solver, then record the observable, reference and use in calibration. Editions of one work count once. Runs reusing a flow, treatment or benchmark remain linked. We distinguish a restriction on admissible stress, a response to prescribed inputs, and a coupled LES or RANS result. A benchmark of either complete solver isolates a wall-treatment change only with matched controls. A heat-flux comparison has its own physical scope. A channel LES resolving the viscous sublayer without a wall treatment, as in Tsubokura~\cite{Tsubokura2009}, supplies a flow comparison only.
